SMS consent & compliance

How visitors opt in to receive text messages

TextCatch collects explicit, informed SMS consent before any text message is sent. The opt-in flow works as follows:

  1. Visitor starts a chat — the TextCatch widget opens on the business’s website.
  2. Visitor enters their phone number — they provide their name, phone, and inquiry.
  3. Visitor may check the SMS consent checkbox — an unchecked-by-default, optional checkbox is presented. The visitor can submit the form without checking it. The checkbox label reads:
✓ “I agree to receive SMS text messages from this business at the number provided in response to my inquiry. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.”
  1. Only if the visitor checks the box and submits does the system send them a confirmation SMS. The business owner is always notified of the inquiry regardless of SMS consent. Visitors can submit without opting in to SMS.

Program name: TextCatch SMS Alerts

Message frequency: Varies; typically 1–2 messages per inquiry
Opt-out: Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe

Consent requirement: Consent is not a condition of purchase or service. The checkbox is unchecked by default and is completely optional — visitors can submit their inquiry without checking it.
